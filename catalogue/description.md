This pack is FFmpeg ported to Phorepoc, built as its own library (`pheffmpeg.dll`, LGPL 2.1 or later) that the video
player and the software video decoder plug-in load at run time. With it, Phorepoc plays H.264, HEVC, VP8 and VP9 video
and AAC, MP3, Opus, Vorbis and FLAC audio in MP4, Matroska and Ogg files.

Some of these formats are covered by patents in some countries, which is why the pack isn't part of any Phorepoc
image. Whether to install it is up to you.
